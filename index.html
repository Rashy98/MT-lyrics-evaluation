Evaluation of Personalised Children's Lyrics

Thank you for taking part in this evaluation.

This study evaluates AI-generated personalised lyrics for children's songs. The lyrics have been generated using information about an anonymised child's age, interests and target words.

What you will do

You will be shown a series of generated lyrics together with a brief anonymised child profile. For each set of lyrics, please provide your professional judgement using the rating scales.

Rating scale

1 = Very poor

2 = Poor

3 = Moderate

4 = Good

5 = Excellent

There are no right or wrong answers. Please base your ratings on your professional judgement.

The order of the lyrics will be randomised. Information about the model or generation method will not be shown during the evaluation.

Estimated time

The evaluation is designed to be completed relatively quickly. You may also leave optional comments where you feel additional feedback would be useful.

Participant code

Please enter the participant code provided to you. This allows your responses to be grouped without requiring your name.
